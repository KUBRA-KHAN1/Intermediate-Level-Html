Our Academic Departments

MUST University offers various academic departments to provide students with quality education and professional skills. Each department focuses on theoretical knowledge, practical learning, research, and career development. Students can choose their departments according to their interests and future goals.

1. Software Engineering

The Software Engineering Department focuses on software development, programming, web development, databases, and software design. Students learn how to develop reliable and efficient software applications.

2. Computer Science

The Computer Science Department provides education in programming, algorithms, computer systems, artificial intelligence, and data science. Students develop problem-solving and computational skills.

3. Electrical Engineering

The Electrical Engineering Department focuses on electrical systems, circuits, electronics, power systems, and communication technologies.

4. Civil Engineering

The Civil Engineering Department teaches students about construction, structural engineering, transportation, and infrastructure development.

Mechanical Engineering

The Mechanical Engineering Department focuses on machines, thermodynamics, manufacturing, mechanical design, and engineering systems.
